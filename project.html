Technical Support Case Study 01

Investigating a Device-Specific App Access Issue

FairMoney · Customer Service

The Challenge

A customer reported being unable to access the FairMoney app on their mobile device, although they could successfully access it using another person's device. This suggested a device-specific issue requiring technical investigation.

My Approach

  1. Requested a screen recording showing the error encountered while accessing the app.
  2. Collected details about the customer's mobile device.
  3. Identified and escalated the issue to the appropriate technical team, providing the recording and device information.
  4. Followed up with the team and communicated progress updates to the customer via email to manage expectations.

The Outcome

Provided the technical team with relevant evidence to investigate the issue while maintaining clear communication with the customer throughout the escalation process.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Technical Troubleshooting
  • Escalation Management
  • Cross-functional Collaboration
  • Customer Communication
  • Case Ownership
KYC & Account Support Case Study 02

Resolving a Delayed Account Upgrade Verification

FairMoney · Customer Service

The Challenge

A customer contacted support regarding a delay in their account upgrade. They explained that they had uploaded their utility bill several weeks earlier, but it was yet to be verified.

My Approach

  1. Empathised with the customer and acknowledged the inconvenience caused by the delay.
  2. Collected the necessary details and reviewed the customer's profile using the CRM tool.
  3. Confirmed that the uploaded utility bill matched the address registered on the account and met the recency and document requirements.
  4. Informed the customer that verification would be completed within 24 working hours.
  5. Escalated the case to the KYC team and followed up until verification was completed.
  6. Notified the customer once their utility bill had been successfully verified.

The Outcome

The customer's utility bill was verified, and the customer was updated on the successful completion of the process.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Customer Empathy
  • KYC Verification
  • CRM Management
  • Attention to Detail
  • Escalation Management
  • Case Ownership
